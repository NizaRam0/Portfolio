about

I like fixing things that quietly waste people's time.

I'm a Computer Science student at Beirut Arab University, graduating in 2027. Most of what I build starts the same way: something is manual, repetitive, or fragile, and I want to see if I can make it not be. That's shown up as an AI planner that turns vague goals into actual subtasks, a platform that keeps a clinic's bookings from double-booking themselves, and — before any of that — IT support work at CCIB, where I spent two summers untangling network issues and cleaning up inconsistent SQL data across branches that couldn't afford downtime.

Lately I've been leaning toward software with a civic angle — Lebanon's public sector has a lot of the same "quietly wasting people's time" problems, just at a bigger scale. Long term, I'm working toward building software and automation for people who don't have the time to build it themselves.
